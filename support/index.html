Common questions

Why are there gaps in a recording?

Normal recording does not keep the phone awake. Android sleep or background restrictions may reduce sampling. An open recording record alone does not establish that samples are still arriving.

How is full-charge runtime calculated?

Lab compares models at the same saved reference power. These are constant-power projections toward 0% SOC, not measured shutdown times. Home’s remaining estimate can use a more recent qualified discharge average.

Does the app upload my battery measurements?

The reviewed build processes measurements locally. You choose whether to export and where to save a file. A cloud-backed file provider may sync it. AdMob is not integrated in the reviewed build.

How can I delete my data?

Delete available raw recordings in the app, or clear Android app storage to remove all app-private data. Exported files must be deleted separately at their saved locations. Models and archives do not automatically expire.

What should I save after an unexpected stop?

If available, save Settings → Recording diagnostics → Save diagnostics report, preferably before restarting recording. Note the approximate incident time and app version. Sending the report is optional.

Before reporting an issue

  • App version and Android version
  • Device model and approximate incident time
  • What you expected and what happened
  • Steps to reproduce, if known

Attach logs only if needed. Review timestamps, device information and any sensitive content first. Never send passwords, signing keys or unrelated personal files.
