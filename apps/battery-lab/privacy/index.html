Scope & release status

This draft covers the Battery Lab Android app and this developer website. It describes the reviewed development build, 0.1.34-recording-recovery (build 37). The developer is guest_ranger, with privacy and support contact at battlab.support@gmail.com. The effective date, distribution regions and final release behavior must be confirmed before publication.

The inspected build processes battery measurements locally and has no Google AdMob SDK integration. The advertising section below describes a possible future integration, not current collection. Re-review this policy and the Google Play Data safety form for the exact bundle you distribute.

Locally stored measurements

When you explicitly start a recording, the app stores battery signals available from Android, such as charge level, current, voltage, temperature, charging/connection state, timestamps, sequence and measurement-validity/freshness information. Records and exports can also include device, Android and app-version metadata.

These records support charts, charge/discharge summaries, fitting and runtime estimates on the device. App-private storage also holds models, fitting-input archives, History summaries and bounded recording diagnostics, which can include service events, error details, process-exit evidence and storage/memory information. The diagnostics report does not contain the full raw sample payloads.

The inspected app does not automatically upload these battery records to a developer server. Local storage does not mean that an export or a future advertising SDK has the same data practices.

Exports you choose to save or share

Exports are initiated by you using Android’s file picker. Depending on the export, files may include raw battery records, detailed timestamps, device identifiers/metadata, fitted models, paired raw-input archives or diagnostic information. Saving a file does not itself send it to the developer.

You choose the destination. A cloud-backed document provider may upload the saved file to your account under its own terms. If you subsequently email, upload or share a file, the recipient or service receives it. Inspect and remove unnecessary information before sharing. Once shared, deleting the app’s copy does not delete other copies.

Potential Google AdMob advertising

Not integrated in the reviewed build. A future release may use Google AdMob. If enabled, Google and any configured advertising partners may process IP addresses, advertising or other device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. IP addresses may be used to infer approximate location. Collection depends on the SDK, configuration, consent and applicable requirements.

This is distinct from local battery measurements; this draft does not authorize sending raw battery recordings to advertisers. Non-personalized ads do not necessarily mean no data collection. Before enabling ads, the developer must confirm the SDK and any mediation partners, update this policy and store declarations, and implement required consent and privacy controls. These controls are not claimed to exist in the current build.

Read Google Mobile Ads SDK disclosures, Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information. If ads are introduced, the release policy must explain the actual privacy-options entry point, applicable choices, recipients, transfers and retention. The developer cannot promise deletion of data independently held by Google.

Retention & deletion

  • Raw measurements: retained locally unless you delete them or enable optional automatic raw cleanup. Cleanup is separate from automatic fitting, defaults off in the reviewed build, and requires completed archives and durable summaries. It is not an unconditional 48-hour deletion guarantee.
  • History: the app presents a rolling seven-calendar-day view; this is not a promise that every underlying stored record expires after seven days.
  • Models and fitting-input archives: can remain on the device without automatic expiry. Deleting live raw rows does not delete detached History or paired archives. A dedicated archive-deletion screen is not currently available.
  • Diagnostics: recent events are retained in a bounded journal of two 256 KiB files. Older events rotate out; exported reports remain separately.
  • Remove all app-private data: use Android Settings → Apps → Battery Lab → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Labels vary by device. Export anything you need first; these actions remove local app records and settings.
  • Exported files: remain in your chosen storage until you delete them there, including cloud copies and provider backups. Clearing app storage does not remove them.
  • Support correspondence: if you contact the developer, the receiving email provider and developer may retain the message and attachments. The developer’s provider and support-retention period are pending review before a public contact channel is activated.

For any future advertising, review Google’s policies and privacy controls for third-party retention and deletion rather than assuming local app deletion removes advertising data.

Sharing & your choices

The reviewed build has no automatic developer-server sharing of battery recordings and no integrated advertising SDK. User-selected destinations and recipients can receive exports as described above. No battery-record sales mechanism was found in the inspected implementation.

You control recording, exports and the separate automatic fitting/cleanup settings. You may stop recording without exporting. For privacy questions or applicable access/deletion requests concerning information you voluntarily send to the developer, use the contact channel once confirmed below. The developer cannot retrieve records that exist only on your device.

Security

Measurements are held in Android app-private storage, subject to the device’s operating-system protections. The reviewed app disables Android automatic app backup. Do not assume the app separately encrypts every database or exported file; no such guarantee is made. Device compromise, unlocked access and files saved outside app-private storage can expose data. Keep your device secure and share diagnostic or raw files carefully.

Website hosting & links

This static website includes no advertising SDK, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, contact form or application database. It does not set its own cookies or use browser storage. Language navigation uses ordinary links.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Hosting logs are separate from the Android app’s battery records. External links are governed by the destination’s practices. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Audience & policy changes

This draft does not establish a child-directed service. The intended audience, Google Play target-age declaration and any advertising treatment for minors must be reviewed before release; no age gate or parental-consent flow is claimed.

Update this policy when release behavior changes, including before adding advertising or another data recipient. Publish an effective date and provide any notice or consent required for the change. English and Simplified Chinese versions are provided; both need consistent review.
