Battery Lab

A clearer view,
one recording at a time.

  1. Start a recording

    Normal recording targets 1 Hz while awake. Screen-off sleep can produce gaps; it does not guarantee continuous 1 Hz sampling.

  2. Review the history

    Look at charge and discharge periods, power averages and temperature alongside the available measurement coverage.

  3. Explore the model

    Fit eligible recordings manually or enable optional 48-hour fitting. Both data quality and sufficient coverage are required.

Stored locally.
Exported by you.

Battery measurements and analysis are stored on the device. You can choose to save raw recordings, model results, paired fitting-input archives or recording diagnostics through Android’s file picker.

Exports can contain detailed timestamps and device information. Review a file before sharing it with anyone.

Read privacy policy

Useful estimates, with clear boundaries.

Reported voltage may be cached, sleep can interrupt sampling, and fitted models are not independently measured battery health or laboratory impedance tests. Runtime projections may extend beyond observed charge levels and do not predict the exact time your phone will shut down.

Automatic fitting currently works within one eligible recording session. Combining interrupted logs into one model is deferred. Automatic first-launch recording and reboot recording are not current features.
