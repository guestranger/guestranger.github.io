Using Battery Lab

Battery Lab is a battery monitoring and analysis tool currently in testing. Use it on a device you are authorized to operate, and use exports and other people’s information responsibly. Recording must be started explicitly; available signals and background behavior vary across Android devices.

Measurements & estimates

Android may provide cached, sparse or unavailable readings. Sampling can pause during sleep. Fitting, capacity, impedance and runtime results depend on the input data and model assumptions. They are not a calibrated battery-health certificate, laboratory measurement, safety diagnosis or guarantee of remaining operating time.

Do not rely on the app to determine whether a damaged or overheating battery is safe. Follow your device manufacturer’s guidance.

Your records & exports

You control when recording starts and stops and when files are exported. Save copies you need before clearing storage, uninstalling or enabling cleanup. Clearing app data can permanently remove local records. Files exported to another location remain under your control and that provider’s terms.

The Battery Lab privacy policy explains local measurements, exports and possible future advertising separately.

Availability & changes

Testing releases can contain errors, change features or produce different results after updates. No continuous recording, permanent storage, support response time or uninterrupted availability is promised. Review release information before updating a recording device.
